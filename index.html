AIOS Privacy Policy

Paradigm Golf Group · Effective November 16, 2026

Who we are

Paradigm Golf Group operates golf courses in the Phoenix area, including Dobson Ranch Golf Course, and manages golf courses for other owners. “AIOS” is an internal tool used only by Paradigm staff.

What the app does

AIOS connects to the Meta business assets of Paradigm and the golf courses it manages, through Meta’s Marketing API to:

  • build ads,
  • manage campaigns,
  • and report on ad performance.

Every ad it builds stays paused until a Paradigm staff member approves it.

What data it accesses

Settings and performance figures for the ad accounts, campaigns, ads, Pages and Instagram accounts of Paradigm and the golf courses it manages. The app has no public users and no Facebook Login. It does not collect personal information from anyone.

How we use it

Only to run and measure advertising for Paradigm and the golf courses it manages.

Sharing

We do not sell or share this data. It stays on systems Paradigm controls, and only Paradigm staff can see it. Access keys are stored in a password manager.

Retention

Ad performance reports are kept as business records. Everything else is kept only while it is needed to run those ads.

Data deletion

The app collects no personal data, so there is nothing about you to delete. If you have a request or question anyway, email us at the address below and we will answer within 30 days.
